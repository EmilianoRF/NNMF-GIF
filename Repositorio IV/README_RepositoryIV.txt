README_RepositoryIV

Repository IV consists of one zip folder:

RepositoryIV.zip

which contains 60 samples, each consisting three synchronised files: 1 avi + 2 wav files. Each trio of files is named

[speaker]_[mode of phonation]_[fundamental frequency].avi
[speaker]_[mode of phonation]_[fundamental frequency]_mic.wav  (speech pressure signal)
[speaker]_[mode of phonation]_[fundamental frequency]_EGG.avi  (electroglottogram)

- 5 male speakers: M01-M05
- 5 female speakers: F01-F05
- mode of phonation: normal or breathy
- fundamental frequency: low, medium or high
